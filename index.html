THE FLOW

Three steps from “where is that file?” to done.

PeerDrop keeps the transfer path visible, intentional, and easy to control.

01

Find a nearby device

Start discovery to see available PeerDrop devices on your local network. Connected devices and new discoveries are kept distinct.

02

Pair when you want trust

Use a one-time verification code to pair devices. Discovery alone does not verify ownership, and pairing can be revoked later.

03

Choose, review, send

Select one or more files, choose one or more recipients, review the request, then watch the transfer progress.

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

A clearer way to share.

Does PeerDrop upload my files to the cloud?

PeerDrop is designed for direct device-to-device transfers over the local network. It does not require a central cloud upload step for the current sharing flow.

Can I send more than one file?

Yes. The send flow supports multiple files and multiple recipients, followed by a review step before the transfer begins.

Is every discovered device trusted?

No. Nearby discovery only makes a device visible. Trust comes from pairing and the approval controls you configure.

Can I pause or cancel a transfer?

Yes. Active transfers support pause, resume, and cancel controls at both transfer and file level where applicable.

Is online mode available now?

Not yet. Online mode is coming soon. The current product is focused on nearby local-network sharing.
